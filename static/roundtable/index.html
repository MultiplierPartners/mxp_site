02Why now

Agents are multiplying faster than they're being governed.

Agentic AI is moving from pilot to production faster than most security programs can govern it. The gap isn't awareness. Most leaders can explain what their agents are for. Far fewer can define access boundaries, revocation timing, or delegation once those agents are live.

04The framework

Seven essentials of responsible agentic security.

The building blocks that show up again and again at the enterprises furthest along the adoption curve. The roundtable is organized around them.

01

Agent identity & lifecycle

Every agent gets its own identity, provisioned just-in-time and retired when the task ends.

02

Delegated, scoped authorization

Every action traces back to who delegated it, scoped to that task alone.

03

Tool & resource access control

Tool calls are first-class access decisions, not implicit trust.

04

Runtime, context-aware policy

Access adapts to real-time context and intent, not permissions fixed at deployment.

05

Human-in-the-loop checkpoints

Sensitive or irreversible actions require explicit approval.

06

End-to-end observability

Every hop to a tool, backend, or other agent is logged and traceable.

07

Credential & cost hygiene

Short-lived, verifiable credentials and spend limits that catch runaway agents early.

Beyond the baseline

Where the leading edge is moving

Owner-set authorization terms, single-use grants, and cryptographic identity over network trust. Discussed in the room.
